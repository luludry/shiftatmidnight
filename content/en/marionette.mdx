## How to Survive Marionette

Listen for the distinct wood-creaking sound when Marionette approaches the window. Lock the bulletproof-glass Security Shutters immediately after hearing it. Marionette will leave on its own after a few minutes.

## How to Identify Marionette

Marionette walks with a very stiff posture. As it gets close to the window, you can hear clear creaking sounds from wood rubbing together. Its ID photo usually shows a face with no expression.
