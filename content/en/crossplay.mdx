## How Many Players Can Join?

Shift at Midnight supports 1-4 players online. One player hosts the session. Up to three friends can join.

## Which Platforms Support Shift at Midnight?

The confirmed platforms are PC through Steam, Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox Cloud Gaming through Xbox Game Pass. The crossplay information also specifically identifies PC Game Pass players. The game is not available on PS5 or Nintendo Switch.

## Is Shift at Midnight Crossplay?

Crossplay is limited. Xbox console players can play with PC Game Pass players. Steam players currently cannot play with Xbox players through crossplay.

## Proximity Chat and Split-Screen

Shift at Midnight includes proximity chat. Voices get quieter as players move farther apart, and voice communication disconnects during monster attacks. Local split-screen is not supported.
