## How to Save Game in Shift at Midnight

You cannot trigger a manual save in Shift at Midnight. Progress saves automatically after the full current shift is complete. The save occurs when you reach the 6:00 AM results screen.

## What Happens If You Quit Mid-Shift?

If you are killed during the shift, all progress from that night is lost. Force-quitting with Alt+F4 has the same result. The next session starts that shift again from its beginning.

## How Multiplayer Saves Work

In multiplayer, story and day progress are saved locally only for the host. Guests retain only their personal achievements and character appearance cosmetics. Choose the player with the most stable network connection to host.
