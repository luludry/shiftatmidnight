## How to Identify the Norbert Impostor

The real Norbert wears his signature red baseball cap. His eyes are blue. If the cap is missing or his eyes are black, the visitor is an impostor posing as Norbert.

## What to Do When Norbert Is an Impostor

Memorize the real Norbert's profile details. Refuse service immediately when his cap or eye color does not match. Then press the silent alarm.

## Who Is the Real Norbert?

Norbert is a regular customer. He comes in every day to buy coffee. His red baseball cap and blue eyes are the profile details used to distinguish him from an impostor.
